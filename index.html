FAQ

  • Fix Desk — your own dedicated desk with 24/7 access, all common areas, internet, refreshments and monthly meeting-room and printing credits.
  • Fix Desk Premium — everything in Fix Desk, plus your desk in a lockable office and reception of mail and parcels.
  • Hot Desk — flexible seating anywhere in the communal areas during opening hours, with access to all common areas and member services.

Members receive day-to-day operational service — regular cleaning, plant care, servicing of coffee machines and printers, restocking of consumables, reception services and support from the coworking team. Membership also includes community activities and selected benefits.

Jurkovičova Tepláreň is located within the SKY PARK complex. You can come on foot, by car or by public transport. If you arrive by car, you can park in the underground garage for €3 / hour. For easier orientation, take a look at our navigation maps or short videos showing the route by car and on foot.

Members have access to fully equipped community kitchens, lounge zones, phone booths, a shower, cycling facilities, meeting rooms of various sizes, and spaces for workshops, training and events. Storage space and parking can also be arranged on request.

Jurkovičova Tepláreň is more than a coworking. Beyond flexible desks, offices and meeting rooms, you also get professional service, smart app-based access and the backing of an established business community. The space is run by Alto Real Estate, so you can expect stability, quality and a standard that goes beyond ordinary coworking.
